Problem

Notices are everywhere. Eligibility is not.

Students miss vital opportunities not from lack of listings, but from the exhausting cost of manual reading.

PAIN 01

Fragmented Document Formats

  • Scattered across PDF guidelines, HWP attachments, image posters, and bulletin HTML.
  • Unstructured binary formats that search engines and keyword filters cannot parse.
Bottleneck: Format Incompatibility
PAIN 02

Buried Qualification Clauses

  • Crucial constraints ("under 29", "no 4th years", "IT majors only") hidden deep inside multi-page briefs.
  • Students must inspect every page just to discover they are disqualified.
Bottleneck: Information Opacity
PAIN 03

Exhausting Reading Overhead

  • Cross-checking academic status, age, and major takes 15–30 minutes per notice.
  • Evaluating 20+ opportunities requires hours of repetitive, manual toil.
Bottleneck: Time & Cognitive Burden
PAIN 04

Fragmented Channels & Missed Deadlines

  • Notices spread across campus portals, Wevity, and ContestKorea without sync.
  • Crucial application deadlines pass unnoticed with zero personalized tracking.
Bottleneck: Opportunity Loss
