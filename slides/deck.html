User Journey

From zero to application in 4 steps

A frictionless 2-minute workflow from profile setup to finalized application decision.

STEP 01 · 1-MINUTE ONBOARDING

Profile Creation

  • Multi-Channel Input: Certificate (UIE), GitHub/blog link, manual fields, or short note (Solar).
  • Unified Storage: Merges university, year, status, skills, and interests into a single profile.
Deliverable: Verified Student Profile
STEP 02 · MULTI-CHANNEL INGEST

Notice Collection

  • Flexible Ingest: Drag & drop PDF / HWP files, paste announcement URLs, or 1-click crawler.
  • Automated Crawling: Direct collection from Wevity and ContestKorea with HWP extraction.
Deliverable: Multi-Modal Ingestion Pipeline
STEP 03 · PERSONALIZED RANKING

Feed & Fit Scoring

  • Instant Verdicts: Live eligibility badges (Eligible Review Ineligible) on every card.
  • Solar Pro 4 Fit: Tailored fit score (0–100) with personalized match rationale.
Deliverable: Intelligent Ranked Opportunity Feed
STEP 04 · PREPARATION & ACTION

Execution & Doc Chat

  • Review Checklist: Ambiguous clauses flagged for human verification before apply.
  • Context Handoff: 1-click transition to Document Chat with source file attached and prompts filled.
Deliverable: Verified Application Submission
